Set up in seconds

Three simple steps to transform your browser.

01

Add to Browser

Navigate to the Chrome Web Store and click "Add to Chrome". Then pin the extension to your toolbar for easy access.

Web Store Link arrow_forward
02

Activate the Mic 🎙️

Navigate to Extensions Settings, click on "Details" under AI Browsing Assistant ✨, choose 'Site settings', and select 'Allow' for Microphone access.

03

Sign In & Go

Authenticate with your Google account through the automatic pop-up page. Your own personal AI assistant is now ready.
